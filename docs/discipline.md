# Discipline doctrine

1. Vellum alone owns workspace membership, documents, source revisions, edits,
   history, saves, persistence effects, and retention.
2. Every federated workspace has one authoritative home. Replicas are read-only.
3. Source disclosure is explicit and profile-scoped; metadata negotiation never
   implies source or history disclosure.
4. Transport identity is provenance, not edit, read, save, or persistence
   authority. Current Vellum policy gates every operation and transfer.
5. Exact replay, changed-input conflict, definitely-not-executed, and
   outcome-unknown remain distinct.
6. Synchronization acknowledgement is not read, edit, save, persistence,
   review, attention, authorship, or semantic-index freshness.
7. Imported events are never re-exported as authoritative home events.
8. Humans and agents traverse identical edit and disclosure laws.
9. Semantic peers, MCP clients, harnesses, Chronos tasks, and Hermes delivery
   remain separate roles and authority domains.
10. No remote production claim exists before disclosure, recovery, adversarial,
    platform, source-artifact, and commit-pinned integrated gates pass.
